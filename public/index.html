How to read these numbers

  • All statistics are computed from the trades in SQLite, on the server, every time you load or reload this dashboard. Nothing here is hard-coded.
  • The headline is focused on today. The "Today" card shows how the current day is going, and the "Best time to trade" comes from your trades on this weekday (e.g. all your Mondays) — so it answers "when should I trade today?" instead of pointing at a different best day each week.
  • Real and demo are always separate. They are never blended into one series, and each mode is compared against its own baseline.
  • Win rate = wins ÷ ALL trades of that mode (breakevens count in the bottom of the fraction, so they pull the win rate down).
  • A bucket needs at least 3 trades before any statistics are shown — otherwise you see "Not enough data yet", never a blank space.
  • Win rates come with a 95% Wilson confidence interval — a range the true win rate is likely to sit in. Wide intervals mean few trades; treat those numbers with caution.
  • Expectancy (average P&L) is shown next to win rate: a high win rate does not automatically mean profitable. When you log stakes, ROI % (total P&L ÷ total stake) is also shown, which removes position-size history from the comparison.
  • "Best time today" is honest, not a promise. It requires at least 5 trades on this weekday, a win rate more than 5 percentage points above that weekday's baseline, and a 95% confidence lower bound above that baseline. The card always states how many trades the hint is based on, and marks small samples.
  • Sample sizes under 30 trades are always flagged as provisional.
  • Timestamps are stored in UTC and bucketed in the fixed configured timezone (shown in the header); manual entry is interpreted in that same timezone.

Persistence: every trade is written straight to data/trades.db on the server. Close the app, restart the server, reopen weeks later — your history is still there. Back up anytime with npm run backup.
