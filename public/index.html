How to read these numbers

  • All statistics are computed from the trades in SQLite, on the server, every time you load or reload this dashboard. Nothing here is hard-coded.
  • Real and demo are always separate. They are never blended into one series, and each mode is compared against its own baseline.
  • A bucket needs at least 3 trades before any statistics are shown — otherwise you see "Not enough data yet", never a blank space.
  • Win rates come with a 95% Wilson confidence interval. Wide intervals mean few trades — treat those numbers with caution.
  • Expectancy (average P&L) is shown next to win rate: a high win rate does not automatically mean profitable.
  • Best window = at least 5 trades of that mode, win rate more than 5 percentage points above that mode's baseline, ranked by the lower bound of the Wilson interval — not by raw win rate.
  • Samples under 30 trades are always flagged as small samples.
  • Timestamps are stored in UTC and bucketed in the fixed configured timezone (shown in the header).

Persistence: every trade is written straight to data/trades.db on the server. Close the app, restart the server, reopen weeks later — your history is still there. Back up anytime with npm run backup.
